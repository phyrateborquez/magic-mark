Magic Mark: highlight what matters as you read.

Turn selected text into temporary yellow highlights. Mark several paragraphs and keep independent highlights in different tabs.

Select text and click the Magic Mark button or use its context menu. The menu lets you undo the latest highlight or clear the document. Prefer a shortcut? Assign your own in your browser: none is assigned by default.

Highlights disappear when you reload, leave the document or close the tab. They can also disappear if your browser discards a tab to save memory. There is no history, synchronization or recovery.

No accounts, advertising or analytics. Magic Mark processes selections locally, does not save your selections and does not send data. It works when you request an action, without continuously monitoring pages.

For selectable HTML text on ordinary web pages. Built-in PDFs, internal or protected pages, text in images and editors are excluded. Some external frames may be inaccessible. Dynamic pages may lose highlights when replacing text.

A free, community, nonprofit, open-source project under MIT. The current interface uses Spanish labels.
